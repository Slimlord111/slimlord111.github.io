Hello, I am the creator of this site, William Brownlee. This site serves as my Archive of all that I've learned in the industry and on my own as a Web developer and DIY engineer. I draw on my years of professional experience, gained as both a student of Computer Engineering and Computer Science, as well as my personal experience as a 3D printing enthusiast. My trials and tribulations, every mistake and realization noted and logged.

On a personal level, I'm a passionate engineer always seeking new avenues to express my technical fantasies. As mentioned above, I've ventured into the realms of web development and FDM 3D printing, but I've also dabbled in robotics, game development, neural network development, and SEO management. I don't just do what I do simply for the money, but for satisfaction, as I love my craft. I wish to grow from and with it, which takes time and dedication.
